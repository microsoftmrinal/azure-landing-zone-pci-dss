What IS a Landing Zone? 🤔
🛬🏗️☁️

It's Like Building a Town!

Before you build houses (apps), you need:

  • 🛣️ Roads (networks) so things can move around
  • 🚔 Police stations (firewalls) to check who comes in
  • 🏦 A bank vault (encryption) to lock up treasures
  • 📋 Rules (policies) everyone must follow
  • 🔑 A guard at every door (identity checks)

A Landing Zone is all of this — built BEFORE any apps arrive!

The Drawbridge 🌉
🌍➡️🚪➡️🏰

Only ONE Way In!

Imagine your castle only has ONE door. Everyone who wants to come in MUST use that door.

At that door sits a super-smart guard called "Web Application Firewall" (WAF). This guard checks every single visitor:

  • ✅ "Are you being nice?" → Come in!
  • ❌ "Are you trying to trick us?" → GO AWAY! 🚫
  • 🤖 "Are you a sneaky robot?" → BLOCKED!

The Fire Moat!

The Azure Firewall Premium is like a moat of fire around our castle. It inspects EVERY message that comes in or goes out.

It even has a lie detector (called IDPS) that can spot when bad guys try to disguise themselves! 🕵️

If it sees ANYTHING suspicious → 🚨 BLOCKED AND REPORTED!

The Secret Vault 🔐
⚙️

Super-Locked Treasure Chest!

Credit card numbers are like treasure 💎. We lock them with a special key that lives inside a Hardware Security Module (HSM).

Think of it as a magic box that:

  • 🔑 Creates super-strong keys
  • 🙈 NEVER lets the keys leave the box
  • 💣 Destroys itself if someone tries to break in!
The Invisible Castle 👻
🛡️

You Can't Attack What You Can't See!

Our castle is basically invisible on the internet! 👀

All our computers, databases, and secret vaults have NO public address. It's like having a house with no street number — mailmen (hackers) can't find it!

Website visitors can only use our front door guard (Front Door). Our partners come in through their own private VPN tunnel. Everything else is hidden behind "Private Endpoints" and a "Private Link Service" — secret tunnels only our own people know about! 🕳️

Super ID Check! 🪪
👤🔑📱✅

"Prove You're REALLY You!"

To get into our castle, you need TWO things (Multi-Factor Authentication):

  • 1️⃣ Something you KNOW (password)
  • 2️⃣ Something you HAVE (a special hardware key you tap! 🔑)

Even if a bad guy steals your password, they STILL can't get in without your physical key! It's like needing both a password AND a magic wand 🪄

Plus: Nobody has permanent admin power. You have to ask permission every time (PIM), and it expires like a movie ticket! 🎬

Two Castles! 🏰🏰
🇳🇱⚡🏰↔️🏰

Always a Backup!

What if our castle gets hit by a storm? ⛈️

We built TWO identical castles in different countries:

  • 🏰 Castle 1: North Europe (main castle)
  • 🏰 Castle 2: West Europe (backup castle)

If Castle 1's server stops answering, the front door guard sends new visitors to Castle 2. Other things (the partner VPN tunnels, the job the app is doing, and the secret keys) each have their own switch-over steps, and we practise them so we know how long they really take. 🎉

The Rule Book 📜
📋🤖🚨

Robot Police Officers!

We have robot police (Azure Policy) that watch EVERYTHING 24/7:

  • 🚫 "Someone tried to make something public!" → DENIED!
  • 🚫 "Someone forgot to encrypt!" → DENIED!
  • 🚫 "Someone tried to build in the wrong country!" → DENIED!
  • 🚫 "Someone forgot to put a name tag!" → DENIED!

These robots NEVER sleep, NEVER take breaks, and NEVER make mistakes. They prevent bad things from even HAPPENING! 🤖💪

The Watchtower 👁️
👁️📊🚨🦸

Sees EVERYTHING!

Our watchtower is called Microsoft Sentinel. It's like having a guard with super-vision who watches every single thing that happens:

  • 👀 Who logged in? When? From where?
  • 👀 What did they touch?
  • 👀 Is someone trying the same door 100 times? (Brute force!)
  • 👀 Is someone moving too much treasure at once? (Data theft!)

If ANYTHING looks weird → 🚨 ALARM! The security team gets called immediately!

The Report Card 📝
🏆

PCI DSS = The Ultimate Test!

PCI DSS is like a super-strict school test for anyone who touches credit cards. It has 12 big rules:

1️⃣ Build strong walls 2️⃣ No easy passwords 3️⃣ Lock up card data 4️⃣ Encrypt everything moving 5️⃣ Fight viruses 6️⃣ Build things safely 7️⃣ Only let the right people in 8️⃣ Check everyone's ID 9️⃣ Lock the physical doors 🔟 Write down everything 1️⃣1️⃣ Test your walls often 1️⃣2️⃣ Have a security plan

Our Landing Zone passes ALL 12! ✅✅✅✅✅✅✅✅✅✅✅✅
